Demo 2 · AI Content Review

A second pair of eyes for every page

Write your style guide down once. The AI checks a page against it, gives it a score, points to each mistake and suggests a fix. It never changes the page by itself.

  1. Choose which pages to check
  2. Write your house rules in plain English
  3. The AI gives a score and points out each mistake
  4. An editor decides what to change

Why it matters for councils

  • 30 editors, 1 style guide, checked the same way every time
  • Plain English checked before a page goes live
  • Find the pages that need the most work
  • It only runs when someone asks. Nothing changes without an editor.
