Christmas And New Year Bin Collections!!

Please note that due to the Christmas & New Year Bank Holidays, collections will be one day late from Monday 21st December - Friday 1st January.

In order to utilise the garden waste service, put your brown bin out by 7.00am on the morning of collection, otherwise we will be unable to empty it and you will need to wait until the next scheduled collection which may well be in the new year.

Real trees can be recycled, e.g. left next to your green bin. For more information click here.

CONTAMINATED BINS WILL NOT BE EMPTIED.

  1. Headings in sentence case
  2. No exclamation marks
  3. “Please note” adds nothing
  4. Write “and”, not “&”
  5. “bank holidays” in lower case
  6. “21 December”, not “21st”
  7. Use “to” for ranges, not a dash
  8. “1 January”, not “1st”
  9. “In order to” → “To”
  10. “utilise” → “use”
  11. “7am”, not “7.00am”
  12. Over 40 words: split it
  13. “for example”, not “e.g.”
  14. “Click here” tells you nothing
  15. No block capitals
