Ask an assistant to update the website

AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot can now use a website's tools, through a universal plug called MCP.

I've built a free LocalGov Drupal add-on, localgov_mcp, that gives an assistant the everyday jobs a web editor does.

Think of it as giving a temp a staff login, not the keys to the building.
