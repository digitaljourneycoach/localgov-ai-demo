Stop 3 · LocalGov MCP

Talk to your website

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is like a universal plug. It gives AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot one standard way to use a website's tools.

I built localgov_mcp, a Drupal module that gives an AI assistant the tools for everyday LocalGov editing jobs.

Think of it as giving a temp a staff login, not the keys to the building.
